# ACI 50-row pilot method approval

## Decision

The study owner approves using the Azure Container Instances replacement method for the 50-row sequential, non-counted technical pilot. The approved ACI U6/U8 definitions are the cross-container-group Blob marker procedures described in `15-ACI-SESSION-METHOD-OVERHAUL-DRAFT.md`.

## Scope

This approval permits preparing and validating the 50-row technical pilot. It does not convert pilot observations into thesis results or waive the separate deployment, isolation, verifier, credentials, U6/U8, or evidence-protection readiness checks. The pilot must stop on an indeterminate result and preserve its record.
