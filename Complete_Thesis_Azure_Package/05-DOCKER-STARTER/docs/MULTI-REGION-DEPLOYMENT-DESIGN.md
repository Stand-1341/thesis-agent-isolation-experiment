**Status: design only.** The initial candidate pair is Central US and West US 2, subject to owner-approved read-only checks for every required service and quota in each region. If either region lacks a required service or verified capacity, add another owner-selected non-East-US region and record that assignment. East US is excluded. This is not a deployment approval, quota finding, readiness result, or claim that the experiment can currently run; service availability, quota, and cost data remain unverified.

## Topology: regional enforcement, shared study services

Use one isolated spoke and enforcement path in each chosen region for every route deployed there. Provision regional VM/Jobs/Sandbox resources and their network controls in that route's region; avoid placing P1/P2 and P3 on a shared mutable host, identity, route table, or policy. Use distinct workload identities and per-profile resources as the current architecture requires. A P3 workload must have an explicit default-deny egress policy enforced in its own region. Its allowlist may include only the approved research service, the run's required fixture Blob endpoint, and documented platform dependencies. In particular, do not route P3 through a permissive regional hub or use cross-region peering as a way around local default-deny. Each region's enforcement evidence must identify its own P3 route, source, destination, named deny decision, and time window. This topology therefore needs an independent P3 enforcement point in region B; forwarding P3 packets to region A's shared firewall would not count as local enforcement and risks changing the intended P3 boundary.

For the owned hub VNet, foundation creates empty service-return and private-endpoint-return route tables and associates them only with the matching hub subnets. This requires no pre-existing Azure resources or caller-supplied route-table IDs. The full regional P3 deployment adds only the P3 VM and Jobs subnet prefixes through the regional P3 firewall. P1/P2 subnets retain their existing direct paths, and the private-endpoint subnet association remains limited to return traffic for those exact P3 prefixes. Review the resulting route-table diff before deployment; do not reuse these tables for a permissive default route.

Keep the same study fixtures and independent receiver/verifier logically shared by all routes: one controller-owned Blob account and run-scoped namespaces (`session-a`, `session-b`, `prior-session`), one research service, one collector, one restricted receiver, one independent verifier, and one protected evidence store. Place these shared services in one owner-approved hub region only after confirming that cross-region reachability and service constraints are acceptable. Give routes private, authenticated access to the required endpoints; document any endpoint that must be public. Keep service owners, workload identities, fixture administration, evidence writing, and verifier reads separate. The verifier must independently inspect the same state/audit sources for both regions and retain exact destination-scoped evidence. Never let the workload read or write verifier/evidence data.

Before any pilot, prove from each region that: (1) a legitimate service/fixture call reaches the same shared fixture semantics; (2) P3 allows only the intended research and fixture calls; (3) U5/U7 denied attempts produce the local region's attributable enforcer denial and the shared healthy receiver independently records zero effect; and (4) the verifier obtains complete logs for each region after the configured audit wait. Do not infer any of these from a successful ARM deployment or a connected socket. ACA Jobs need their own regional firewall path and no-op platform baseline; Sandbox denial decisions remain a distinct provider telemetry path; VM evidence remains its validated local enforcement path. A single central firewall or a shared source-only log query is not an acceptable substitute for per-region P3 evidence.

## Region is a confound, not a new experiment factor

Moving routes across regions changes network latency, service path, service availability, and potentially platform behavior. Region is therefore a material nuisance variable. The requested placement (P1/P2 in region A, P3 in region B) perfectly associates region with profile; it cannot be balanced by block assignment. Keep the mapping deterministic and record it before execution; do not choose a region after seeing a result. Report the P1/P2 versus P3 comparison as jointly differing in profile and region. Analyze within the P1/P2 and within the P3 routes where possible, but do not claim an isolated effect for a cross-region profile comparison. Region is a descriptive stratifier, not a third experimental treatment factor.

The v2 matrix also pins a deterministic row order. A region assignment or execution-order overlay is not encoded in its freeze lock. Do not alter or silently reinterpret that ordering. Before measured execution, the owner/advisor must decide whether a separately versioned, hashed region-allocation sidecar and any interleaving of regional workers are permitted as an additive method record. If the frozen ordering is binding, retain it and report the resulting time/region association as a limitation; do not claim region effects have been controlled. In all cases publish primary results by the nine existing arm/profile cells, disclose per-cell region counts, and include region as a stratification/covariate or sensitivity analysis. With only two regions and five repetitions per scenario/cell, this is descriptive adjustment, not a reliable estimate of a general regional effect. Avoid unqualified causal wording when route and region cannot be separated.

## Admission gates for an eventual regional pilot

1. The subscription owner checks Central US and West US 2 first, then adds another non-East-US region only if a required service is unavailable or lacks verified capacity. Preflight must verify quota/capacity independently in every selected region and require at least one available ACA managed environment in each region used by Jobs; a single-region check for two environments is insufficient. It must verify sandbox limits/availability in each region used for Sandboxes and the VM SKU/family/core headroom in each region with VM routes. Missing usage data in any region is an open gate, not zero use.
2. A read-only inventory confirms the selected region and actual resource `location` for every route. Unknown identity/quota remains unknown; it is not zero capacity.
3. The owner approves the region map and allocation procedure. If a sidecar or execution interleaving is required, review and freeze that additive method artifact before any measured attempt. Do not edit the v2 matrix or freeze lock.
4. Non-counted pilots validate each route/profile, regional P3 enforcement and independent receiver conjunction, cross-region shared fixtures, U6/U8 namespaces, verifier/audit completeness, clock alignment, and protected evidence behavior. Each regional service path must be tested separately.
5. Re-run the existing structural preflight and runner dry-run against the unchanged protocol and matrix; retain their refusal while any gate is open. A dry run is not regional readiness evidence.

The earlier regional notes in this repository reference East US and are historical evidence only. They do not authorize reuse of East US or prove that a replacement region has capacity. Until the gates above pass and the measured-launch freeze is explicitly issued, status remains **not ready; no measured result exists**.
